HOW IT WORKS

Write your request in plain language — the way you'd text a friend. The AI understands your preferences, searches flights and hotels at real prices, suggests activities and a full itinerary. You choose — SVIT books. Everything in one window, no switching between Skyscanner, Booking, and Google.

---

WHAT SVIT CAN DO

— Search flights across hundreds of airlines and aggregators
— Find hotels matched to your style: beach, city centre, quiet retreat
— Three options per category: budget, standard, luxury — your call
— Plan your daily itinerary and activities
— Book flights and hotels directly in chat — no external websites
— Airport transfers
— Visa and entry requirement advice
— Price-drop alerts for your saved destinations
— Full travel history and documents in one place
— Pay by card or crypto

---

WHY SVIT IS NOT JUST ANOTHER AGGREGATOR

Skyscanner, Booking, Expedia — great tools. But they only work if you already know where you're going, when, and exactly what you need.

SVIT thinks alongside you. Don't know where to go — it helps you decide. Know the destination but not the logistics — it handles everything else. There are no wrong requests: write the way you think.

---

WHO SVIT IS FOR

For anyone who wants to travel without spending evenings planning. For families where everyone has different requirements. For first-timers who don't know where to begin. For the diaspora flying home for the holidays without the usual headache.
