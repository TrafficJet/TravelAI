Symbol Anatomy

S-Path — Upper Lobe

M 32 32
C 32 20, 44 20, 44 14
C 44 8, 32 8, 32 13

Starts at pivot. Sweeps north-east, hooks north-west. Reads as outbound route heading.

S-Path — Lower Lobe

M 32 32
C 32 44, 20 44, 20 50
C 20 56, 32 56, 32 51

Mirror arc. Sweeps south-west, hooks south-east. Completes the compass-needle S-form.

Compass Ring + Ticks

circle r="28" sw="1.5"
4 × tick, len=5.5, sw="1.5"

Bounding frame. Optional at sizes below 32 px. Monochrome at same colour as S-path.

Centre Pivot

circle cx="32" cy="32" r="2.8"

Anchors the two arcs. "You are here." At 16 px favicon this dot is the primary readout.
